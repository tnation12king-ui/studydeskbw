      Before you start

      Can't see your course? Request it and the most requested courses are added first.

      Is Study Desk free?

      Yes. You enter your name and email once to open it, and everything inside is free. Early users may get a discount if paid features are added later.

      Which universities and courses are covered?

      Seven University of Botswana first-year courses today: CHE 101, COM 141, ICT141, MAT111, PHY112, PSY 101 and MGT 100. BIUST, BUAN, BAC and Botho University are next. Request your course and it moves up the list.

      Where do the notes and questions come from?

      Notes are written from lecture slides and course material. Questions come from past tests and exam papers, sorted by topic, plus new questions written so that every topic is covered. Every calculation is worked out and checked.

      Is this an official university website?

      No. Study Desk is an independent study resource. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the University of Botswana or any other university. Always check your own course outline for what will be examined.

      What happens to my details?

      Your name and email are sent to Study Desk's sign-up form with the time of each visit. Your scores, ticks and mock results stay in your own browser and are not sent anywhere.

      I found a mistake. How do I report it?
